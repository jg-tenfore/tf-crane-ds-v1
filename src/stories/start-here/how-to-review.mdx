<Section title="What to look at on each page">
<Grid cols={3}>
    <NavCard eyebrow="Type" title="Is the hierarchy clear?">
        Does the page title, then the section, then the detail read in that order? SF Pro on iPhone, Outfit on the web.
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="Layout" title="Does it hold at every width?">
        Try 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 in the review panel. Anything cramped, cut off or too far apart?
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="Booking" title="Is booking clear?">
        Can you tell which times are open, what a round costs, and how far ahead you can book?
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="Checkout and tax" title="Would you trust it?">
        Is it clear where the order ships or is picked up, and where the tax is charged?
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="Copy" title="Does it say the right thing?">
        Labels, buttons and messages. Is anything missing, unclear or wrong for a golfer?
    </NavCard>
</Grid>
