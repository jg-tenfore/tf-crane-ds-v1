<Section title="The principle" intro="Courses pick from things we've already made look good. They never start from a blank canvas.">

<Grid cols={3}>
    <NavCard eyebrow="1" title="Curated, not typed">
        Twelve tuned palettes instead of a hex-code field. Every palette is contrast-checked in light and dark, so buttons, links and badges always read.
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="2" title="Guardrails built in">
        The app adds a shade behind text on photos, crops to a focus point, warns about low-resolution photos, and falls back to a TenFore photo set when there isn't one. It never falls back to a logo.
    </NavCard>
    <NavCard eyebrow="3" title="Set in Buck, seen by golfers">
        Courses choose in an "App appearance" page with a live phone preview. Golfers just get their course's look. They don't pick themes.
    </NavCard>

<Section title="Our recommendation">

<Callout title="Start small, keep it curated.">
    Ship palette, hero photo, logo + name and the feed, self-serve in Buck with guardrails, with a TenFore review on first setup and on anything that needs an App Store release (icon, splash). Offer "done for you" setup to clubs that want it. Revisit layout presets and fonts once a few clubs are live and we can see what they actually change.
</Callout>

</Section>
