## 1 · Reuse Fox's web layouts

**The issue:** use Fox's web layouts, with their checkout flows and edge cases, instead of designing CraneWeb from scratch.

**Status:** built in Crane Web.

**How it's addressed:** the shop and checkout in Crane Web are the Fox layouts, restyled in Crane and wired to CraneWeb's routes. That covers Shop all, Product, Cart, Add more, Checkout, Confirmation and Restaurant. Fox's edge cases came with them: sold out, expired card, empty cart. Fox itself stays an archive.

## 2 · Browse by category on mobile

**The issue:** on mobile there's no way to browse by category. It needs a menu at the top, or a search.

**Status:** built in Crane Web.

**How it's addressed:** below 768px the shop has a sticky bar under the header. It has a search field, a **Categories** button and a row of category chips. Categories opens every group and subgroup, with item counts. Inside a group, the chips switch to its subgroups. The Restaurant menu has the same search and chips.

## 3 · Shipping address, and tax where it ships

**The issue:** checkout needs a normal shipping address field, so tax is charged where the item ships. Tax varies by state, county and city. This came up on a call with a university course.

**Status:** designed in Crane Web. The tax work needs sizing (see Timing).

**How it's addressed:** checkout asks **Ship** or **Pick up at the course**.

- **Ship:** pick a saved address or add a new one with a normal address form. The tax row names the destination and its rate, and it recalculates when the address changes.
- **Pick up at the course:** taxed where the course is ({COURSE_TAX.label}).
- **Before an address is chosen:** tax reads "Calculated after address".
- **Food & drinks:** pickup only, so they are always taxed at the course. Food and pro shop items check out separately.

Sample rates in the prototype (real rates would come from a tax service):

## 4 · Keep Fox's deep links

**The issue:** keep Fox's deep links, so a button on the course website or in an email can open a specific page. For example Beer, or `/shop/golf-balls/dozens`.

**Status:** built in Crane Web.

**How it's addressed:** every level has its own URL, and the address bar always shows it.

    <thead>

    <tbody>
